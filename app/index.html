One playthrough, about 200 hours, no online trophies and no mission replay. The order below keeps backtracking to a minimum: finish the story first, then grind the 100% checklist, then sweep up the strangers and the odd jobs. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off the save you use for the platinum. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots, 1 autosave) as insurance.

Difficulty 4/10 and about 200 hours, per PowerPyx. The hardest trophy is A Long Road, the gold for 100% completion. Sources: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap and GTABase — GTA 6 Trophy & Achievement List.

Only one trophy in the whole list can be missed. Everything else stays open in free-roam after the story, so relax and play the story first.

Often feared, but safe

  • Story trophies cannot be missed. Every chapter and named-mission trophy pops as you play; there is no point of no return for any of them.
  • Character questlines come back. Kenton goes quiet for a stretch after Chapter 1 and returns in Chapters 2 to 3. Andy, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret all stay available after the story.
  • Optional missions that expire do not matter. Many purple and black-with-pink-letter missions disappear after a while, but the 100% checklist only needs 100 of them and there are far more than that.
  • Fences that vanish do not matter. Some fences become permanently unavailable, but new ones unlock as you go, and no trophy needs a specific fence.
  • Magazine collectibles in story buildings do not matter. They also spawn in other locations, and no trophy needs all of them.
  • Your criminal profile is not a trap. Only the Irredeemable profile is permanent, and it only makes cops more aggressive; it blocks no trophy.
